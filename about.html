About us

Your global education partner

Hamraah Advisors (SMC-Private) Limited is a study-abroad consultancy registered in Pakistan, built around one promise: real guidance from people who have lived the journey.

Our name

Hamraah means fellow traveller.

Studying abroad is one of the biggest journeys a student and their family will make. We believe nobody should make it alone, or rely on advice from people who have never made it themselves.

Our idea

Guidance from people already living your dream.

Hamraah connects students in Pakistan with Pakistani graduates, professionals and students in the USA, who share real, first-hand experience at every step.

Our vision

To be Pakistan's most trusted companion for studying abroad.

A consultancy students and parents recommend to their friends, because we were honest with them from day one.

Our mission

Clear, practical advice, delivered with care and complete honesty.

We make admissions, finances and visas simple to understand and act on, and we stay accountable for the help we give.

The difference

Real guidance, not just application processing

A typical consultancy

  • One general agent for every student
  • Advice second-hand, from people who never studied abroad
  • Fees and "guarantees" that change later
  • Support ends when the visa is stamped

Hamraah

  • You choose the expert who fits your goals
  • Guidance from Pakistanis living and working in the USA
  • Written agreement, clear prices, no false promises
  • Support continues after you land
