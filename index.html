Questions

Frequently asked

Who are the experts?

Pakistani graduates, working professionals and current students living in the USA. Each one has been through US admissions and the F-1 visa process personally, and we verify their background before they join.

Can you guarantee my visa?

No, and nobody honestly can. Visa decisions are made only by the US embassy or consulate. What we do is make sure your application, documents and interview preparation are as strong and as genuine as possible.

How do sessions work?

Choose a session type and, if you like, an expert. We confirm a time that suits both of you and send a Zoom or Google Meet link.

Which countries do you cover?

Our main focus is the USA. We also guide students applying to selected countries and universities in Europe. Ask us about your destination.

What does it cost?

It depends on the session or package you choose. We share every fee in writing before you pay, with no hidden charges.
